HEART RATE

Zones use 60/70/80/90% of maximum HR. Resting HR is optional and improves the post-workout VO₂max estimate.

These watch controls are locked until the active workout finishes. Targets below can still be changed live.

WORKOUT TARGET

RunLog shows the equivalent speed during rides. Brief GPS fluctuations are ignored before an alert.

RUN NAME

Date/time codes (strftime): %Y year, %y 2-digit year, %m month, %d day, %H hour, %M min, %S sec, %F date (YYYY-MM-DD), %T time (HH:MM:SS), %R HH:MM, %a/%A weekday, %b/%B month name, %p AM/PM, %j day-of-year, %D US date, %% literal %. Extras: {activity}/{Activity}, {part}/{Part} (morning/afternoon/evening/night). ({date} and {time} still work.) Blank = default %Y-%m-%d_%H%M_{activity}.

FILE FORMAT

GPX and TCX upload anywhere. FIT is binary — ideal for Strava/Garmin/intervals, but the destination must accept .fit.

UPLOAD

Uploads are manual — press Upload on the watch debrief, or "Upload now" on an activity below. Nothing leaves your phone on its own.

RunLog logs in to your instance itself and refreshes the token automatically when it expires. Credentials stay in the Pebble app on this phone.

Settings and per-activity actions apply when this page closes. Download buttons work without closing.
